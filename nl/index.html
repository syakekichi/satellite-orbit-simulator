SatViewer3D User Guide & Legal Terms

🖱️ 3D Navigation Guide

  • Mouse Wheel / Touch: Silky smooth ultra-fine zoom in/out.
  • Left Drag: Rotate Earth in full 360-degree freedom.
  • Right Drag / Ctrl + Drag: Tilt camera and change pitch angle.
  • Click / Search Satellite: Select satellite to view live altitude, speed, and collision risks.
  • 🎯 Track Camera: Auto-lock camera on moving satellite.
  • 🔮 Space Debris Risk: Real-time 24h orbital crossing analysis (MOID).
